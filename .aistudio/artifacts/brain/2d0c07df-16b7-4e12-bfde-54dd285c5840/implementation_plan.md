# Results Layout Restructuring: Final Ratio Repositioning & 5-Minute Increment Ratios

## Overview of Completed Changes
1. **Ratio Graphic Placement Directly Below Educator Name**:
   - The primary OTR ratio graphic (`Opportunities to Respond per minute: ...` and `Goal: 3-5 ...`) is placed directly below `Observed Educator Name: [Name]` at the top of the report.
   - The Instructional Feedback ratio graphic (`Average Positive to Negative (ratio): ...` and `Goal: 5:1 ...`) is placed just under the Opportunities to Respond ratio and directly above the Notes section.
   - For multi-block reports, subsequent blocks also present their respective ratio and goal below their block title.
   - Removed duplicate ratio and goal lines from the bottom of raw data sections.

2. **Engagement Check Results Repositioned**:
   - Moved the `Engagement Check:` section (Partners, Individual, and minute-by-minute off-task counts) to appear immediately below `Time length:` (and curriculum/target questions if present), and directly above the `Whole Group:` raw data.
   - Removed the duplicate engagement check blocks from the bottom of OTR and Instructional Feedback modes.

3. **5-Minute Increment Ratios**:
   - Added a `Five-Minute Increment Ratios:` section directly under the raw data of Whole Group, Small Group, and Instructional Feedback.
   - Renders 5-minute intervals matching the chart logic (`0-5 min ratio`, `5-10 min ratio`, etc.).
   - Rendered with lighter pastel pill styling: soft pastel green/red badges with subtle 1.5px borders (`#86efac` / `#fca5a5`), clean metric text (`#15803d` / `#b91c1c`), and lighter row highlight backgrounds (`rgba(240, 253, 244, 0.4)` / `rgba(254, 242, 242, 0.4)`).
